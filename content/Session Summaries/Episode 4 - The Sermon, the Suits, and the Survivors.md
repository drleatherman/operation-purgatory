# Episode 4: The Sermon, the Suits, and the Survivors

Date: 2026-07-02

## Summary
At six o'clock, the party gathered near the hanging gardens on the fifth floor of the Beta building, where cascading hydroponic vines heavy with vegetables and fruit lined the walls. Tucked into a recessed nook that had once served as a storage area, a small congregation had already assembled. An elderly gentleman with gray, wispy hair and a cream-colored frock introduced himself as Father Fresco and welcomed the group as fellow pilgrims, asking each person to share an act of humility from their day. He preached a cautionary sermon against envy, warning that it turned friends into foes and love into hatred, and spoke of a pilgrimage through seven holy gates — each one capable of cleansing a sin forever — that would ultimately lead the faithful to true enlightenment. He explained the history of the Aidai, an ancient civilization that had once flourished across the planet before it stopped rotating, killing most of their kind, and whose final writings described this sacred ritual of purification. Father Fresco revealed that a recent planet-quake almost certainly meant one of the gates had been opened, and he offered to quietly download classified maps of the ancient Adai sites to the party's personal tablets.

The meeting was cut short by the sound of heavy boots striking the floor. Two Loss Prevention officers, tasers in hand, marched toward the nook with unmistakable purpose. A tear gas grenade was hurled toward the approaching officers, billowing out in a thick, choking cloud — but the only exit from the nook led straight through the gas, and the entire group, party and congregation alike, was caught in the fumes. Father Fresco, elderly and slow, was overcome by the gas and had to be hauled upright by two members of the party, who threw his arms over their shoulders and dragged him toward the elevators. One of the officers, retching and half-blind, still managed to seize Eudora by the ankle as she ran past, but she wrenched free with a sharp kick and sprinted on.

The party piled into an elevator, only to find it already occupied by a silent figure in a skin-tight rubber suit bearing a thoughtful expression, emanating a foul, sauna-like odor. The figure stepped aside without a word to make room. When the elevator doors opened on the sixth floor, the party was met with a sight that stopped them cold — roughly twenty more individuals in identical rubber suits, their faces stamped with exaggerated, unsettling expressions, who immediately surged toward the elevator. Several jammed their rubbery hands into the doorway to force it back open, and the cramped car became a scene of desperate, clumsy violence. Scalpels slashed at the rubber suits but bounced off uselessly, a rifle was fired into the crowd and punched through multiple figures, and a revolver was pressed directly to the head of one attacker, bringing the group to a momentary, trembling halt. A well-placed kick finally sent the figure blocking the doorway stumbling backward, and the elevator doors slid shut, leaving two rubber-suited figures trapped inside with the party.

With a gun still pressed to its head, one of the rubber-suited figures grew still and began to speak in a quiet, exhausted voice. It explained that they had awoken in a lab with no memory of how they had gotten there, already encased in the rubber suits, and had been forced to survive a gauntlet of lethal puzzle rooms — including one where a red circle on the floor split anyone who stepped in it cleanly in half. Of the fifty people who had started, only twenty made it out alive, and they had been told they could only leave if they found replacements. The party realized, with a sinking feeling, that they had been shooting and stabbing a group of desperate, traumatized survivors who were simply trying to escape. Both figures stood down, and the party agreed to help them.

The group retreated to Ennis's tidy corporate apartment on the seventh floor, where Reginald was sprawled on the couch, flipping through a magazine. The party set to work with scalpels, carefully peeling the rubber suits away from the two survivors, a process that proved gruesome — the material had partially fused to their skin, leaving raw, splotchy patches wherever it tore free. As the rubber was peeled back from one survivor's face, it became clear that one of his ears had been completely torn away, the wound long since healed over. The man introduced himself as **James**, and **Reginald**, looking up from his magazine, recognized him — they had been on the same reconnaissance mission near the sun line, surveying an ancient structure on a high peak facing the sun fields. **James** explained that corporate routinely erased the memories of workers who stumbled upon significant discoveries, which was why Reginald remembered nothing of his return — and why he was missing fingers he couldn't account for. James, who had kept his memories intact, described how Reginald had disappeared into one of the rooms during the mission and returned white as a sheet, and he offered to draw a map to the location using the discarded remnants of his peeled-off rubber suit as a writing surface, pressing the details of the ancient structure's whereabouts into the skin-flecked rubber with careful, deliberate strokes.

## NPCs
### Father Fresco
An elderly gentleman with gray, wispy hair wearing a cream-colored frock. He leads the Church of the Last Scion, preaching a message of humility and enlightenment through a pilgrimage of seven holy gates. He is incapacitated by tear gas during the escape from the hanging gardens.

### Reginald
A jumpy, malnourished man with slicked-back chestnut hair who is missing several fingers. He is hiding in Ennis's room and suffers from memory loss regarding his return from a reconnaissance mission near the sun line, likely due to corporate memory erasure.

### Loss Prevention Officers
Two corporate security personnel wearing heavy boots and carrying tasers, who march into the hanging gardens to break up the Church of the Last Scion meeting. One manages to grab Eudora's ankle despite being caught in tear gas before being kicked away.

### James
A former operations worker who was trapped in a rubber skin-suit after surviving a series of lethal puzzle rooms in a mysterious lab. He is missing one ear, which tore off during a mission near the sun line. He retains his memories of the reconnaissance mission he shared with Reginald and offers to draw a map to the ancient structure they were investigating.

### Rubber-Suited Figures
A group of approximately twenty individuals wearing skin-tight rubber suits with exaggerated, emoji-like facial expressions and a foul, sauna-like odor. They are survivors of a deadly lab who were told they could only leave if they found replacements, leading to their desperate and clumsy assault on the elevator.

## Locations
### Hanging Gardens
A naturally lit, open room on the fifth floor of the Beta building filled with rows of hanging hydroponic gardens and cascading vines growing vegetables and fruit. A recessed nook in one corner, originally meant for storage, serves as the secret meeting place for the Church of the Last Scion.

### Beta Building Elevator
A standard transport lift that becomes the site of a claustrophobic and violent confrontation with a crowd of rubber-suited individuals rushing from the sixth floor.

### Ennis's Room
A tidy corporate dwelling on the seventh floor of the complex, featuring a white couch, bare bookshelves, and a hidden safe. It serves as a temporary safe house for the party and their growing collection of refugees, including Reginald and the two rubber-suit survivors.

## Items
### Tear Gas Grenade
A tactical device purchased by a party member and deployed in the hanging gardens to create a billowing cloud of irritating gas. It successfully incapacitated the approaching Loss Prevention officers but also caught the party and the church members in its effects.

### Tasers
Electroshock weapons carried by the Loss Prevention officers who arrived to break up the Church of the Last Scion meeting in the hanging gardens.

### Scalpel
A sharp surgical instrument used by party members both as a weapon against the rubber-suited attackers in the elevator — with limited effect, as it bounced off the rubber — and later as a tool to carefully peel the fused rubber suits off the survivors.

### Revolver
A sidearm brandished during the elevator confrontation. A shot fired during the scuffle missed its mark due to being jostled, but pressing it to an attacker's head was enough to bring the rubber-suited figures to a standstill.

### Rifle
A long-range firearm fired at close range into the crowd of rubber-suited attackers in the elevator hallway, puncturing holes through the suits and wounding multiple figures.

### Rubber Skin Suits
Unsettling, second-skin-like rubber coatings with exaggerated facial expressions that were fused to the bodies of the lab escapees. They emit a foul, sauna-like odor and required painful surgical removal, leaving the wearers' skin raw and splotchy.

### Discarded Rubber Map
A makeshift map drawn by James onto the remnants of his peeled-off rubber suit — still bearing bits of his skin — detailing the location of an ancient structure on a peak near the sun line where he and Reginald were originally compromised.

### Personal Tablets
Electronic devices carried by the party members. Father Fresco offered to download classified maps of the ancient Adai sites onto them, though this was interrupted before it could happen.
