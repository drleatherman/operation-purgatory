## Full Summary
The party arrived at the spire to find it inhabited by a religious cult calling themselves the Penitents of Wrath, led by a man named Goddard — a former expedition cook whose foot had been replaced by a grafted hand, giving him three hands in total. A thick, smoky haze hung at waist level throughout the facility, the air tasted of blood, and the walls were smeared with it. In the living quarters, desperate figures clawed and squeezed themselves into wall vents, as if trying to escape something unseen. Goddard welcomed the party with an unnerving calm and led them deeper into the spire, explaining that they followed the path of the Scion and that the Last Prophet had once spoken to them through the facility's computer systems — until the Prophet's final message warned that the gates of Envy had been opened, and then fell silent forever.

Goddard brought the party into the prayer room, where an eight-foot-tall statue dominated the chamber. It was a grotesque sculpture made entirely of severed arms, legs, and hands, hewn and ripped and assembled into the rough shape of a figure. One eye socket was a black, bottomless pit; the other held a single one-inch ruby that caught the dim light. Goddard called it Gabriel. A voice then spoke directly into the minds of those present, promising the secret of the universe to anyone willing to offer a sacrifice — something truly dear to them, whether a limb, an emotion, or something of deep personal meaning.

Tabatha stepped forward first and offered her apathy to the statue, feeling it drain out of her through the soles of her feet as the ruby eye flashed deep red. A door on the far side of the room melted away before her eyes, revealing a hidden chamber with bright, reflective steel walls and a three-foot-tall holographic chalice on a pedestal, bathed in white light and filled with a sticky, blood-red liquid that smelled, strangely, of pudding. She drank from it and felt something shift inside her — a clarity, a new perception, as though patterns in the world had suddenly become visible to her that had always been hidden. Oneida followed, sacrificing his wisdom teeth, which held deep cultural significance as symbols of purity and ancestral memory. Goddard extracted them with a pair of pliers, and the four teeth rolled across the floor and up into the face of the statue on their own, absorbed into Gabriel's grotesque form. Oneida drank from the chalice and felt the same awakening wash over him, his inner eye opening to a world layered with deeper meaning.

James, however, could not bring himself to part with any piece of his inner self. He confessed to the party that Gabriel had haunted him for some time — that every third face he saw had empty eye sockets, that hands crawled across his floor in the night, and that he had once woken with one wrapped around his throat. Driven past his breaking point, he drew a knife, screamed and sobbed, and slowly sawed off his own left hand, hurling it at the statue before sprinting out of the room. He was found moments later in the reception area, frantically tearing apart the room until he found a small handheld blowtorch, which he used to cauterize the stump in a scene of agonized, desperate relief.

Ennis and Mike Jackson had seen enough. Rather than submit to Gabriel's demands, they decided to destroy the idol entirely. Ennis pried the ruby from the statue's eye socket with his bare hands, leaving two hollow, staring pits of darkness. The party gathered defaced magazines and wooden crates from the prayer room and stacked them at the base of the statue, building a bonfire. As the flames caught and began to climb, a voice thundered in their minds: they had chosen death. Goddard came rushing back into the room in a panic, trying to pull the kindling away, but Ennis shoved him back and opened fire, cutting him down before he could save his idol.

Four mutated cultists — Penitents of Wrath, with mismatched body parts grafted onto them in grotesque configurations — burst into the prayer room to stop the desecration. The fight was brutal and chaotic. Ennis managed to wound and then kill the lead attacker, but one of the cultists possessed terrifying psychic abilities, projecting invisible walls of force with devastating effect. Mike Jackson was struck by one such blast and hurled across the room into the far wall, where he crumpled and did not rise again. In his final moments, something of Mike Jackson lingered — a silhouette of the man, glimpsed beyond a wash of white light, and the sealed door to the grail room melted away before Ennis, a last gift from a dying crewmate.

Ennis ran to the grail room and drank from the chalice, feeling the same awakening that had come to Tabatha and Oneida. He returned to the fight just as a new figure emerged from the living quarters: Jack Michaelson, a prisoner who had been held by the cult and had resisted making any sacrifice, enduring the nightmares rather than submit. He fired his revolver at the cultist dragging Ennis toward the bonfire, and the bullet ricocheted off the wall and buried itself in the attacker's shoulder, forcing them to drop Ennis. The psychic cultist launched another shockwave at Jack, but some instinct — or perhaps something deeper — told him to step aside, and the force passed harmlessly by. The two men fought back together, and in a moment of poetic justice, the psychic cultist tripped at the edge of the bonfire and fell into the flames, where Jack finished them off with a shot from his nail gun.

Two rusted industrial androids then rolled in from the reception area, one of them spraying water in an attempt to douse the growing fire consuming Gabriel. Ennis, his back broken from an earlier psychic blast, collapsed into unconsciousness. James rushed to his side and drove a stimulant needle into him, pulling him back from the edge of death. Jack navigated to the HVAC room — a stone-carved chamber filled with industrial air scrubbers, rust-stained ducts, and flickering hazard lights — and found a central control console with a large red emergency button. He slammed it, and every android in the facility went instantly inert. The fire continued to burn, and the blackened, shriveling remains of Gabriel toppled from its dais and collapsed onto the floor.

As the party caught their breath and gathered what weapons they could from the fallen, the illusion concealing the grail room door faded entirely, and a deep, distorted voice crackled to life over the intercom. It mourned its dead children, warned of an approaching apocalypse, eternal death, and asked for an emissary to visit Tyr's Final Wish — before cutting out into silence. In the HVAC room, Ennis and Jack noticed a stone archway had opened in the northern wall, carved into the mountainside itself, leading somewhere deeper into the dark.

## NPCs
### Goddard
A former expedition cook who joined the Penitents. He is missing a foot, which has been replaced by an extra hand, making him three-handed. He serves as a guide for the party within the spire, assists Oneida in extracting his wisdom teeth with pliers, and is ultimately shot and killed by Ennis when he tries to stop the party from burning the Gabriel statue.

### Gabriel
A grotesque, eight-foot-tall sculpture made of hewn-off arms, legs, and hands, standing on a central dais. It features one empty eye socket and one containing a one-inch ruby. It communicates telepathically, demanding sacrifices of great personal value in exchange for the secrets of the universe. It is ultimately destroyed by the party's bonfire.

### The Last Prophet
A mysterious entity that speaks to the cult through the spire's computer systems. Its last message claimed the gates of Envy had been opened before it went silent. A distorted voice later crackles over the intercom mourning its 'children,' warning of apocalypse, and demanding an emissary to discuss a final wish.

### James
A former cook for the expedition who is haunted by terrifying visions of crawling hands and staring eyes. In a fit of desperation, he severs his own left hand to appease Gabriel and cauterizes the wound with a blowtorch. He later saves Ennis from death by administering a stimulant injection.

### Psychic Cultist
One of the four attackers who possesses supernatural mental powers, capable of projecting invisible walls of force. They killed Mike Jackson and broke Ennis's back before being driven off.

### Penitents of Wrath
Cultists with various body parts grafted onto them in odd configurations. Some possess supernatural strength. They ambushed the party in the prayer room, armed with shivs and a gun.

### Fixer Bots
Rusted but functional industrial androids, including a 'Squirt Maker 5000' engineer model designed for repairs and fire suppression. They were shut down remotely by Jack Michaelson via the HVAC room console.

## Locations
### The Spire
A mysterious structure inhabited by a religious cult called the Penitents. The interior is filled with a strange smoky haze, the air tastes of blood, and blood is smeared across the walls.

### The Prayer Room
A dimly lit chamber within the spire featuring a central dais where the grotesque limb-constructed statue of Gabriel stands. It has a short knee-high wall along the outside with a walkway circling it, a few picked-over crates, a security camera, and a hidden door that only appears to those who have made a sacrifice. It becomes the site of a large bonfire and a deadly skirmish with cultists.

### The GRAIL Room
A hidden chamber with bright steel walls that reflect light in a dizzying fashion, accessible only through a door that melts away for those who have made a sacrifice. It contains a pedestal holding a three-foot-tall holographic chalice filled with a sticky, blood-red liquid.

### Reception Area
A room filled with a thick, smoky haze hanging at waist level where James frantically searched for a tool to treat his self-inflicted wound after severing his own hand.

### Living Quarters
An area filled with overturned bunks and smeared blood where people were seen desperately trying to jam themselves into wall vents. Jack Michaelson was being held prisoner here by the cultists.

### HVAC Room
A stone-walled chamber carved into the mountainside, containing banks of industrial air scrubbers, rust-stained ducts, flickering hazard lights, and a central control console with a large red emergency shut-off button. Two deactivated androids were docked here.

### The Infirmary
A base the party previously visited where the inhabitants were quarantined due to a highly contagious sickness. A muscular man with a railgun attacked the party's Warthog as they fled.
