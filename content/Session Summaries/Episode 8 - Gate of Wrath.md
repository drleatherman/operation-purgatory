# Episode 8: Gate of Wrath
## Summary
The party descended a flight of slick, film-covered stairs through a towering stone archway that had opened in the HVAC room of the spire. Jack Michaelson carried the broken-backed Ennis on his back as they made their way down into a black iron chamber decorated with bas-reliefs of angels frozen in various poses. At the center of the room stood an android with a screen for a head, flanked by two deactivated androids missing their screens. The screen displayed the image of a blue-skinned humanoid with long features, glowing blue eyes, and wisps of blue and red smoke curling around their face — the Last Prophet — who welcomed the party to his home.

The Last Prophet spoke at length about the world outside, lamenting the loss of Aidar's once-lush green landscape and mourning the corruption of his adopted children by a terrible plague known as the Body Politic. He explained that the virus had been designed as a biological cure for tyranny — a genetic liberation — but had catastrophically backfired, causing each limb and muscle to act independently of the mind's commands. Those infected were driven by an insatiable compulsion to spread the virus to others, shoving pieces of themselves onto the uninfected in a desperate, mindless urge to propagate. The Prophet revealed that his old acquaintance, known as Zephyrus and mythologized as the Last Scion, was believed to be researching a cure at a place called Tyr's Final Wish — but to reach it, the party would first need to pass through a series of ancient gates.

To open the Gate of Wrath that loomed behind the Prophet, two ruby gemstones — the gems of sight — needed to be placed into the empty eye sockets of the stone angel carved into the archway. The party had recovered one ruby from the effigy of Gabriel, but a second was still missing. Leaving Ennis in the chamber, the others retraced their steps through the spire, searching the bodies of the fallen penitents. Among the dead, they found an ornate wooden cudgel with a tassel, a set of soft black leather straps resembling a corset alongside a thin leather choker, and a paper-wrapped package stuffed into a corner. Ennis, ever impulsive, tore the package open and inhaled deeply — immediately identifying the contents as a massive quantity of cocaine — and entered a state of frenzied, hyper-focused overdrive. The second ruby was eventually found clutched tightly in the cold fist of one of the dead penitents.

On their way back, the party encountered James — a terrified, one-handed survivor who had been lingering among the bodies. They brought him along, but when they returned to the black iron room, the Last Prophet immediately noticed James's missing hand and recognized the telltale signs of infection. James grew agitated and aggressive, demanding to be let through the gate, his behavior growing increasingly erratic. With little hesitation, Ennis raised his nail gun and fired, dropping James to the floor. His body was dragged out of the room, and the grim business was done.

With James dealt with, the party turned their attention to the gate itself. Jack Michaelson stepped forward and released his revolver — the weapon he had used to escape his captors — letting it clatter to the stone floor as a quiet, deliberate act of surrendering his anger and vengeance. Ennis, for his part, let go of the deep resentment he had carried toward Oneida and Tabitha for their perceived abandonment. The two rubies were then pressed into the empty eye sockets of the stone angel. The gems flashed and began to smolder with a deep ember glow, and from one eye poured tears of blood while from the other streamed rivulets of shimmering light. The liquids pooled at the base of the archway and flowed into a glowing semicircular design etched into the floor. When the design filled completely, the carved angel vanished and the archway transformed into a shimmering curtain of light.

The party stepped through and emerged into Celestia — a breathtaking realm of white marble walkways flanked by towering pillars, floating islands drifting overhead with waterfalls cascading off their edges into open air, and a great mountain adorned with rainbows stretching in every direction. In an amphitheater nearby, a choir of middle-ranked angels with broad white feathered wings and colorful sashes sang a melody so pure it reverberated through the bones of everyone who heard it. The music washed over the party, mending Ennis's broken back, relieving the deep stress they all carried, and leaving cool, glowing marks in the shape of the letter P traced onto their foreheads like ink drawn with light. One of the angels stepped forward and welcomed them, explaining that they had passed through the Gate of Meekness and were now on a pilgrimage of purification.

The angel warned the party gravely that those who sought Celestia for the wrong reasons were legion, and if such individuals ever found their way through the gates, the realm would reflect back a twisted, corrupted version of itself — sending waves of destruction washing over Aidar and purging everything in sight. The choir provided what information they could about the locations of the other ancient shrines and gates scattered across Aidar, noting that many might now lie underground after millennia of geological shift. Then the heavenly realm shimmered and blurred, and the party found themselves back in the black iron room, standing before the Last Prophet once more. He congratulated them, explained that the marks on their foreheads signified the gradual purification of their souls, and confirmed that only once all the gates had been passed would the doorway to Tyr's Final Wish become visible to them. With directions to the remaining gates and a rough bearing toward where Zephyrus was last known to be, the party made their way out of the spire and into the burning fields of Purgatory, the path ahead long and the gates still waiting.

## Short
The party descended into a black iron chamber beneath the spire, where they encountered the Last Prophet — a blue-skinned humanoid displayed on an android's screen. He explained the history of Aidar's devastation, including a biological plague called the Body Politic, originally designed as a cure for tyranny but now causing infected individuals to compulsively spread the virus by grafting pieces of themselves onto others. He revealed that an old acquaintance, Zephyros (mythologized as the Last Scion), was believed to be researching a cure at a place called Tyr's Final Wish — but reaching it required passing through a series of ancient gates. To open the first, the Gate of Wrath, two ruby gems needed to be placed into a stone angel's eye sockets. The party retraced their steps through the spire, looting the fallen penitents and recovering the missing ruby — along with a cudgel, leather garments, and a large quantity of cocaine that sent Ennis into a frenzied overdrive. On their return, they encountered James, a terrified one-handed survivor, but the Last Prophet immediately identified him as infected. When James grew erratic and aggressive, a party member shot him with a nail gun, and his body was removed from the chamber.

With James dealt with, the party turned to the emotional requirements of the gate. Jack Michaelson relinquished his revolver — a symbol of his captivity and desire for vengeance — and Ennis released his deep resentment toward Oneida and Tabitha for their perceived abandonment. The two rubies were set into the angel's eye sockets, triggering a cascade of blood and shimmering light that filled a glowing floor design and transformed the archway into a curtain of light. Stepping through, the party emerged in Celestia — a breathtaking realm of white marble, floating islands, and cascading waterfalls — where a choir of angels sang a melody that healed Ennis's broken back, eased the party's stress, and left glowing marks shaped like the letter P on their foreheads. An angel warned them gravely that those who entered Celestia for the wrong reasons could trigger a corrupted reflection of the realm, sending waves of destruction across Aidar. The choir shared what they knew of the remaining gates' locations before the vision faded, returning the party to the black iron chamber. The Last Prophet confirmed that the marks signified the gradual purification of their souls and that all gates had to be passed before the path to Tyr's Final Wish would be revealed. Armed with directions to the remaining gates and a bearing toward Zephyros's last known location, the party departed the spire and set out across the burning fields of Aidar.

## NPCs
### The Last Prophet
An ancient android with a screen for a head displaying the image of a blue-skinned humanoid with glowing blue eyes and wisps of colored smoke. He paces among the angelic statues in the black iron room, acting as guardian of the gates. He mourns the corruption of his adopted 'children' by the body politic virus and guides the party toward the pilgrimage to Tyr's Final Wish.

### Zephyros (The Last Scion)
Also known as Caius and mythologized as the Last Scion, he is an old acquaintance of the Last Prophet who was the first to pass through the gates. He reportedly fell into madness during his journey but is believed to be researching a cure for the body politic virus at Tyr's Final Wish.

### James
A terrified survivor who had previously severed his own hand. When brought into the black iron room, he is identified by the Last Prophet as infected with the body politic virus. He becomes agitated and aggressive before being executed by the party with a nail gun.

### The Angelic Choir
Middle-ranked celestial beings with large white feathered wings and colorful sashes denoting rank. They sing a healing song that mends the party's injuries and purifies their souls, and one steps forward to warn the party about those who seek Celestia for the wrong reasons.

## Locations
### The HVAC Room
A utility area where the party previously deactivated androids and where a mysterious stone archway appeared, leading deeper into the spire.

### The Black Iron Room
A chamber at the bottom of slick, film-covered stairs, featuring bas-reliefs of angels in various poses, a cryo bed, two deactivated androids, and the Last Prophet's android terminal. The thirty-foot Gate of Wrath is set into the north wall.

### Aidar
The planet the party inhabits, once described as lush and green but now a harsh desert landscape filled with caustic acid washes and stunted succulents. The angels refer to it as a former outpost of heaven.

### Tyr's Final Wish
A cordoned-off and isolated meeting place accessible only to those who have passed through all the gates, where the Last Scion Zephyrus is believed to be researching a cure for the body politic virus.

### Celestia
A radiant heavenly realm featuring white marble walkways and pillars, a great mountain adorned with rainbows, floating islands, and waterfalls cascading through the air. An angelic choir performs in an amphitheater here.
