# Welcome to Purgatory

Congratulations on your recent employment with the Abbatoir Family Company. Attached is your welcome letter and the Company Bylaws. By joining the Family, acceptance of the bylaws are implicit, but we must formally request and receive your signature for our records.

I know the circumstances of your employment will take some getting used to, but we've found that the best way to assimilate is to smile at every trial.

You will be stationed in the Purgatory system. You will receive your first assignment when you touch down on the surface. Do not fear the asteroid field surrounding the planet. Cato, the Warden of Gatekeeper Station, has a 0.942325322457 success rate in getting new family members to the surface.
